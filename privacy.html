Terms of Service

OmniDark is a private, self-hosted publishing tool. It runs on its owner's own computer and is used only by its owner to edit short videos and publish them to social media accounts that the owner controls, including TikTok through the TikTok Login Kit and Content Posting API.

Who can use it

OmniDark is not offered to the public. Only the owner and the TikTok accounts the owner connects use the app. You must be at least 18 years old to connect an account.

Your content

You keep all rights to the videos you publish. You are responsible for having the rights or permission to every video, sound and clip you publish, and for following the TikTok Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.

What the app does on TikTok

  • Publishes videos you approved to the TikTok account you connected.
  • Reads basic profile information (display name and account ID) to show which account is connected.
  • Reads view, like, comment and share counts of the videos it published, if you enable metrics.

Disconnecting

You can disconnect at any time from the Accounts screen in OmniDark, and revoke access in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions.

No warranty

The software is provided as is, without warranty. The owner is not liable for posts that fail, are delayed, or are removed by a platform.

Changes

These terms may be updated. The effective date at the top of this page shows the latest version.

Privacy Policy

Data we access

  • From TikTok: your account ID (open_id), display name, access and refresh tokens, and statistics of videos published through the app.
  • The videos and captions you choose to publish.

How it is used

Only to publish your approved videos, show which account is connected, and show performance of those videos to you. No data is used for advertising or profiling.

Where it is stored

All data stays on the owner's own computer, in the OmniDark data folder. Tokens are stored in a local file readable only by the app. Nothing is uploaded to OmniDark servers, because there are none.

Sharing

Data is never sold or shared with third parties. It is only sent to the platform you publish to (for example, TikTok) when you publish.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are deleted when you disconnect the account in OmniDark. Revoking access in the TikTok app also invalidates them. Statistics are kept until you delete the app's data folder.

Children

OmniDark is not directed at anyone under 18 and does not knowingly process data from minors.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the direct messages of the connected channel's profile.
